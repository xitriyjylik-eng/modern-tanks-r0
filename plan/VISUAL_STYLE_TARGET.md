# Modern Tanks — CURRENT VISUAL STYLE TARGET

Updated: 2026-09-11

## Platform
- Sega Mega Drive / Genesis target.
- Native gameplay presentation around 320×224.
- Readable silhouettes and palette discipline are more important than decorative noise.

## World art
- Top-down authored 16-bit pixel art.
- Dense, coherent terrain with roads, rivers, bridges, forests, ruins, buildings, fortifications, craters, fire/smoke and other landmarks where appropriate.
- The world must look hand-composed, not like a repeated procedural tile grid.
- Technical sector boundaries must not be visually apparent.
- Do not simplify the approved Map 01 composition merely to satisfy old R3 metrics.

## Tanks
- Compact readable gameplay silhouettes.
- Clear hull, turret, tracks and gun direction.
- Distinct class silhouettes and palette accents.
- Directional frames must remain readable at gameplay scale.

## UI
- Steel/navy military language consistent with the world art.
- Strong contrast and compact information density.
- HUD must not obscure the playable area unnecessarily.
- No detached decorative elements that compete with gameplay readability.

## Color
- Earth/forest/stone/water/fire colors should remain saturated enough for 16-bit readability while avoiding flat neon blocks.
- UI accents may use cold steel/navy tones with restrained bright highlights.

## Current source of truth
The approved Map 01 visual reference in `references/` plus the current authored Map 01 master/art are authoritative. Old rejected R2/R3 mockups and the old 1024×768 R3 Region 1 are not visual targets.
